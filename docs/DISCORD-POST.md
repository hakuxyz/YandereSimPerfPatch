**🎮 Faster Yandere Simulator in 2 minutes**

Your game keeps stuttering, loading slowly, or just feels sluggish? This patch
fixes that. It is free, it is open source, and you do not need to know
anything about computers to use it.

**What you need**
A Windows PC and the game installed. That is it. No account, no payment, no
settings to figure out.

**Install, step by step**

**1.** Open the release page and download the Windows zip:
https://github.com/hakuxyz/YandereSimPerfPatch/releases/latest

On that page, scroll down to the section called **Assets**, then click
**YandereSimPerfPatch-win-x64.zip**. The download takes a moment, it is
around 30 MB.

**2.** Close Yandere Simulator completely first. The patch cannot touch the
game files while it is open.

**3.** Right click the zip you just downloaded, choose **Extract All**, then
**Extract**. You now have a normal folder.

**4.** Open that folder and double click **inject.bat**.

**5.** A black window opens. Wait until you see something like
`Patched: ...` or `12/12 patch groups applied`, then press any key.
If it says `Already patched`, you are also done.

**6.** Start the game the way you always do. Done ✅

**Windows shows a scary blue warning?**
That happens with new, unsigned files. Click **More info**, then
**Run anyway**. The full source code of this tool is public on GitHub, so
everyone can check that it does nothing bad.

**It did not work?**
Do not stress it. Write in the help channel, someone will walk you through it
personally. There is also a video guide on YouTube that shows every single
click: https://www.youtube.com/@xyzhakuu

**What the patch actually does (short version)**
Students far away do not update every frame anymore, a few heavy graphics
effects start turned off, and shadows cost less when you turn them on.
Your saves, your gameplay, and your files stay untouched, and you can undo
everything with one command.
